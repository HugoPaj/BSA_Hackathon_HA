Swissquote Wallet Challenge · Proposal

Your keys. Your control.
One tap from your Swissquote account.

A passkey-controlled Safe smart account. The client is the only one who can move funds. Swissquote is a recovery helper, never a spender: it can help replace a lost passkey, but only together with a factor the client holds, and only after a delay the client can cancel.

●Passkey signs, no seed phrase ●Swissquote can never spend ●Recovery a thief can't pass ●Gasless USDC ●Compliant sponsored lane ●Web app, any device ●Real transactions on Base Sepolia
The problem

Today a client has to choose between two bad options

Either Swissquote holds the keys, or the client withdraws to MetaMask or a Ledger and leaves the Swissquote ecosystem, taking the assets and the relationship with them.

🏦 Keep it at Swissquote

Custodial
  • Swissquote holds the keys
  • No DeFi, no on-chain use
  • "Not your keys, not your coins"

🚪 Withdraw to an external wallet

Leaves the ecosystem
  • Seed phrase on paper, lost forever if lost
  • Manual wallet-ownership checks for every withdrawal
  • Assets and client activity leave Swissquote

✨ Our third option

Self-custody, bank-adjacent
  • Client holds the only spending key
  • Lives next to the Swissquote account
  • Recoverable without a seed phrase
The solution in 30 seconds

Three building blocks, all built on audited components

We write almost no smart-contract code. Each block uses an established, audited component.

1

Passkey signing

A WebAuthn passkey on the client's phone or computer is the owner of a Safe smart account. Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or the device PIN approves every transaction. The Safe checks the P-256 signature on-chain. The client never sees a seed phrase.

2

Recovery that can't become a takeover

Lost passkeys are replaced by two guardians: Swissquote or a trusted contact, plus the client's own recovery factor. A 3-day delay with alerts on every channel lets the client cancel any recovery they didn't start.

3

Compliant sponsored lane

Swissquote pays the gas, so clients send USDC without ever holding ETH. Before sponsoring, Swissquote runs an allowlist, a simulation and a sanctions screen. It declines to pay for risky transactions but never blocks the client.

Lifecycle

Create, authenticate, sign, lose, recover

Every step happens in one responsive web app that runs on phones and computers alike. The wallet is deployed with recovery switched on before it can receive funds, so no client ever holds money in a wallet that cannot be recovered.

Create

"Open self-custody wallet" next to the Swissquote account. Face ID, Touch ID or Windows Hello creates a passkey. A sponsored first transaction deploys the Safe, enables recovery and registers guardians.

Client · 1 tap

Authenticate

Every signature needs WebAuthn user verification, meaning biometrics or the device PIN. The flag is checked on-chain. No session keys, no passwords.

Client · biometrics

Sign & send

Client sends USDC. The app shows a simulated balance change and fee. Face ID signs, Swissquote sponsors gas, the bundler lands it on Base Sepolia.

Client · real transaction

Lose access

Phone lost and passkey gone. Funds are untouched on-chain. Nobody can spend them, including Swissquote.

Nobody can act

Recover

New device, new passkey. The client's recovery factor and Swissquote approve. After the delay the new passkey becomes owner and the old one stops working.

Client + guardians · 3 days
Recovery deep dive

Two slots, both required. The client always fills one of them.

Recovery never rebuilds the old key. It asks the Safe to swap the owner from the lost passkey to a new one. Guardians can approve that swap but can never spend.

Bonus · gasless & compliant dApp interaction

A sponsored lane: Swissquote pays gas for what passes its checks

Clients send USDC without ever buying ETH. Every request runs the same pipeline before Swissquote agrees to sponsor it.

✓ Passes: sponsored

The paymaster pays the ETH gas. Swissquote's small fee is a second USDC transfer batched into the same transaction, visible on the explorer.

✕ Fails: "Swissquote won't sponsor this"

The client is warned and keeps full control. They may still pay their own gas. Swissquote declines a service, it does not block a transaction.

Why decline instead of block? If Swissquote could stop transactions, regulators could argue it has power of disposal and the wallet becomes custodial. The checks protect Swissquote's own exposure without taking control from the client. On-chain enforcement through a Safe Guard is the production path for clients who opt in to hard limits.
Swiss regulatory considerations

Designed to stay non-custodial, with automated ownership proof

1 · Non-custodial by design

FINMA treats a wallet provider as non-custodial when it has neither legal nor actual power of disposal over client assets. Swissquote cannot sign, cannot replace the owner alone, cannot recover without the client's factor, and every recovery is delayed and cancellable. Clients can also opt out of bank-assisted recovery entirely.

2 · Off Swissquote's balance sheet

Assets in the Safe never enter Swissquote's possession. The custody-segregation rules that protect clients in a custodian's bankruptcy (Art. 242a DEBA) are therefore not even triggered. There is no deposit and no custody capital to hold.

3 · Wallet ownership proof, automated

FINMA Guidance 02/2019 requires proof, by suitable technical means, that an external wallet belongs to the client before transfers to it. Our wallet is deployed for a KYC'd client, who also signs a passkey challenge verified via EIP-1271. Swissquote archives the proof and re-attests it after each recovery. No screenshots, no test transfers.

4 · Screening where Swissquote acts

Swissquote screens what it sponsors and its own transfers in and out of the wallet, as it does today. The sponsored lane adds sanctions screening and simulation without giving Swissquote control over the client's own transactions.

Not legal advice. The custody classification of a guardian role depends on the full technical and contractual setup and needs validation by Swiss counsel and FINMA. Client terms must not give Swissquote any right to start a recovery on its own or to refuse one arbitrarily.
Business case

Keep the assets, earn on activity, cut verification costs

🔒 Retention

Today, self-custody means a withdrawal and lost AUM. Now "withdraw" becomes "move to my wallet", one tap from trading and one tap back.

💶 Revenue

Small fee on sponsored transactions. Swaps and on-ramp from the Swissquote account. Bank-assisted recovery as part of a premium account package.

📉 Lower cost

Automatic ownership proof replaces manual wallet checks. No safekeeping operations or insurance for self-custodied assets. Fewer withdrawal support tickets.

🚀 New clients

Crypto users who want DeFi but fear losing a seed phrase. A Swiss bank behind recovery is something MetaMask and Ledger cannot offer.

Metrics we would validate with Swissquote data

  • Share of external crypto withdrawals redirected to the wallet instead.
  • Value flowing back from the wallet to Swissquote trading.
  • Sponsored transactions per active wallet per month, times the fee, minus gas cost.
  • Manual wallet-verification cases avoided.

Honest limitations

What this design does not solve, and what we would do next

A thief with the unlocked phone can spend

No wallet can tell a thief who knows the PIN from the owner. We limit the damage with sponsored-lane limits today. In production, an opt-in Safe Guard would delay large transfers and let guardians cancel them.

Cloud accounts are in the trust base

Synced passkeys mean an Apple or Google account takeover matters. This also means losing a phone rarely loses the passkey. A high-balance tier would require a device-bound security key.

Delay protects only an attentive client

The cancel window helps against insiders and collusion, but only if the client reads the alerts. That is why we send them through push, e-mail and the e-banking inbox, and by post for large balances.

Privacy and legal validation

Guardian addresses are public on-chain and could link a client to Swissquote. The legal classification needs counsel. Both are open items for a production pilot.
